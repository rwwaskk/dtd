Privacy Policy

Don't Think, Do. helps you keep notes, tasks, routines, and focus timers. This policy explains what the app stores and when data leaves your device.

Information you add

Notes, task progress, categories, routines, and attachments are stored in the app’s local storage on your device. The app does not require an in-app account.

Optional iCloud Drive backup

If you enable iCloud backup or choose to back up or sync manually, the app transfers your notes and attachments to its iCloud Drive container associated with your Apple Account. Apple provides and manages iCloud Drive. The app does not send this backup to a developer-operated server.

Import and export

You can export a backup or import one from a file you choose. You control where exported files are saved and which files you import. Anyone with access to an exported file may be able to read the notes and attachments it contains.

Timers and notifications

With your permission, the app can send a local notification when a focus timer ends. Live Activities and notifications may display a task title and timer information on your device, including its Lock Screen. You can manage notification access in your device settings.

Data sent to us

The app does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking services, and it does not send your note content to a developer-operated server. If you email support, we receive your email address and whatever information you choose to include so we can respond.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual notes in the app. Deleting the app removes its local data from that device, but a backup you placed in iCloud Drive or exported elsewhere may remain. You can manage those copies using your Apple Account and the location where you saved exported files.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date above will change when we make a material update. For privacy questions or requests, email lovelitbud@gmail.com.
