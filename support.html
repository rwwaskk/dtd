Getting started

The app includes example notes. Open one to see how checkboxes, timed tasks, and repeating routines work. You can find a guide under Settings → Formatting Help.

Backups

Use Settings to export or import a backup. Import replaces the notes currently on your device, so keep a copy of anything you want to retain.

iCloud Drive

iCloud backup is optional. If it is unavailable, check that your device is signed in to your Apple Account and iCloud Drive is enabled. Include the exact error message when contacting support.

Reporting a problem

Please include your app version, device model, iOS version, what you expected, and the steps that reproduce the issue. Share note content only if you are comfortable doing so.
